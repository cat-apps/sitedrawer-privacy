SiteDrawer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

SiteDrawer is a free, open browser extension that lets you organize your most-used websites into a toolbar drawer. This policy explains, plainly, what it does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. SiteDrawer does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics or telemetry of any kind.

Where your data lives

The sites and Spaces you create are stored locally in your browser using the extension storage API (storage.local and storage.sync). If you are signed in and have browser sync enabled, that data is synced only through your own browser account (for example Chrome Sync) between your own devices. It is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Permissions

SiteDrawer requests the minimum permissions needed for its features: storage to save your Spaces, tabs to read the current tab's address when you choose to add it and to open your saved sites, contextMenus for the right-click "Add link to SiteDrawer" option, and tabGroups only when you choose to open a Space as a tab group. None of this information is recorded or shared.

Site icons

Tiles show each site's own icon, requested directly from that website. No third-party icon or favicon service is used.
